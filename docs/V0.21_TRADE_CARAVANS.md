# v0.21: Trade Caravans

**Player problem.** v0.19 showed that everything spending goods was a one-off. Past a point goods only piled up, and "Leave your load" was a patch.

**Trade Caravans give goods a repeatable use.** A long game now throws away 64% less: 2,240 units instead of 6,160. Nobody has played it, and "Leave your load" is still needed at the very end, so this reduces the problem without removing it.

## Design

- **Trade Caravan:** 200 Logs and 20 Panels (80 in a normal game), planned in the Projects tab (`B`) as often as you like.
- **Real goods:** the Construction Spirit carries it all to a spot near Main Storage, like any project. When it's finished the caravan leaves, and the spot is free again.
- **Advice:** the storage message offers one when you can pay for it, ahead of "Leave your load".
- **New goal:** Send 5 Trade Caravans, making 13 in all.

Under the hood this is one flag, `repeatable`. The plan, demand, builder and cancelling are the same as for any building.

### What a caravan gives you

Room, and a count toward the goal. There's no other reward yet. The next milestone is what caravans bring back.

### Rejected

- **Road upkeep:** it uses goods too, but it adds a chore that punishes players for using roads.
