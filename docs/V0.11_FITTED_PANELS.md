# v0.11: Fitted Panels

**Player problem.** Roads use 760 planks once. Anyone who keeps training Woodworking, including everyone retraining after a sacrifice, makes planks for hours with nothing left to spend them on.

**The measurements say panels fix this for at least two more hours of hand training.** Training on planks alone stalled with Main Storage full. Training on panels kept going and earned about five times the XP. They also change the pace of the game a lot, and nobody has played it, so that may not be welcome.

## Design

- **Fitted Panels:** 3 planks make 1 panel, at Woodworking level 7, for 90 XP. That's more XP a minute than Precise Planks and less than Structural Beams. It's the first recipe with a finished good as its input.
- **Chosen, never automatic:** `chosen_only` keeps panels out of "best available", so nobody loses planks they were keeping. You choose it in the Training tab (`T`).
- **Balance:** at first I gave panels 110 XP, and the Training tab showed them beating Structural Beams. Beams should stay the top recipe, so it's 90.
- **Spirits can make them too:** panels have a production order, with `Panels +5` in the Production tab, and a plan that needs panels creates demand like anything else.
- **A bench holding planks for panels keeps them:** haulers leave a bench's input buffer alone while panels are wanted, so planks aren't carried back and forth.
- **Panelled Warehouse:** +300 room in Main Storage, twice a Storehouse, up to two. 60 panels and 20 beams in a normal game.
- **Carrying Frames:** every spirit carries 5 more, including spirits made later. 40 panels and 40 planks in a normal game.
- **Advice:** when planks fill the store, the message offers panels once they're unlocked, and a Warehouse once Storehouses are used up.

Together these change what hand training is. Past level 7 the player can eat what spirits make, so spirit output becomes the limit on training.

### Rejected

- **Choosing panels automatically:** it would use up planks a player was keeping for a road.
- **Making Woodworking produce fewer planks:** that would change everything already measured, and solve nothing past level 10.

## Measured

120 minutes of hand Woodworking after the normal route, with the log target at 150. Reproduce with:

```bash
cargo run -p spirit_sim --release --example panel_report
```

| Habit | XP | XP/min | Panels | Main Storage | Stuck with a full bag | Waiting for materials |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planks only | 10,350 | 86 | 0 | 750 of 750 | 5,085s | 1,180s |
| Panels when there's plenty | 53,725 | 447 | 530 | 722 of 1,350 | 0s | 4,528s |

- **Planks only stalls.** The bag is stuck full for 71% of the two hours.
- **Panels keep going,** and the limit moves to materials: the player waits 63% of the time for spirits to bring planks. That's the intended change.
- **The reference player buys both Warehouses and the Frames** in the panel run.

## Things to look at

- **Pace.** 447 XP a minute is over three times the 127 of the v0.9 route. At that rate, Woodworking 10 to 20 takes under five hours. I can't tell if that's too fast. The recipe's `xp` in `defs.toml` is the number to change.
- **Panels pile up too.** After two hours most of Main Storage is panels, and the 160 or so that buildings use are gone. At this rate the larger store fills in a few more hours. They take a third of the room, so this delays the problem and doesn't end it.
- **Plank demand doesn't chain.** A plan that needs panels asks for panels, but doesn't raise the plank order to match. With no planks in storage the panel order waits, and says so.
- **Failure would have been** Main Storage filling with panels within the two hours, or planks travelling back and forth. Neither happened in these runs.

## Saves

- The format is now **version 7**. Older saves gain an order for panels with no target, so nothing changes until the player wants some.
- **Tested on real files:** a fixture from the unmodified v0.10 code with a road under way, plus v0.8 and v0.9 fixtures.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **373 pass**, 353 from before plus 20 in `tests/panels.rs`.
- **Existing tests caught four real bugs:** at level 7, an empty-handed player took planks from storage instead of logs. Outposts held back planks a local worker could turn into panels. Panels pushed "Keep Precise Planks" out of the Soul Separation options, and the Soul preview described a spirit as a panel maker. All four now ignore `chosen_only` recipes unless panels are wanted.
- **Three existing assertions changed:** the bench now has four recipes, a migrated save has two extra orders, and "Keep Precise Planks" now really does give up one recipe, Fitted Panels.
- Three release-only tests pass: the normal route, its room check, and the two-hour comparison.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Fifteen UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_panels.txt`.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- Physical mouse and keyboard.
- **Whether panels are worth choosing to a person, or whether the XP rate is right.**
- Whether anyone finds the recipe without the storage message.
- Whether waiting at the bench for planks is acceptable.

## Technical debt

- `used_at_bench` protects a whole input buffer of planks, even when the bench holds finished planks to send home.
- Buttons for extra orders are named by position in `ui.rs`.
