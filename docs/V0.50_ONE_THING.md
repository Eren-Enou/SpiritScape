# v0.50: One thing at a time

**Player problem.** A new game introduces its systems one at a time: the Soul tab opens when your first spirit is due, Woodworking when the first plank is, Quarrying once the settlement is settled. Most of the panel follows that. Two places didn't.

A minute into a new game, from the `ui_journey.txt` screenshots:

- **The Soul Separation panel offered five skills:** Woodcutting, Woodworking, Quarrying, Masonry and Kilnwork. The player has only ever chopped a tree. Four of those buttons lead to "you have 0 XP".
- **The Progress tab's "Your skills" listed all five,** with their unlocks: Keystones, Bricks, Structural Beams.
- **"Ambitions" pointed to an Outpost Storage "in Projects (B)"** when there was no Projects tab yet, and to Woodworking level 10.
- **The Soul Separation comparison marked two rows "lose"** without saying what would be lost.
- **"Settlement improvements" listed four buildings,** with shortages like "0/20 Planks, 0/6 Structural Beams", before the player had seen a plank.

None of it is wrong, but in the first minute it says "here is how much you don't know" instead of "here is what to do".

**Now the panels only name what you've met.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Soul Separation and "Your skills" only show skills that have opened,** by the same rule the rest of the panel uses. The skill already chosen always shows, so a choice can't vanish from under you.
- **Ambitions show only their count until the Projects tab opens:** "0 of 19 done. Bigger things to aim for come into view once you can plan buildings." Every ambition is about planning or building something.
- **"Settlement improvements" only lists a building in full when you've met everything it's made of,** until the Projects tab opens. Buildings already standing are always listed. So the Training Cache (15 Logs) appears once you've cut a log, and the Storehouse waits for planks.
- **The comparison says what's lost:** "lose Oak", or "lose Oak +1" when there's more than one thing.
- **Scenarios still show everything.** They're for testing one system, and start partway through a game.

No rules changed, only what's shown.

**What would count as failure:** a skill the player has XP in that they can't separate, a hidden Ambitions list after Projects opens, or a building the player is asked to build that the panel won't show.

## Two bugs the new screenshots showed

Once the improvements list was short enough to read, two things in it were plainly wrong:

- **Oak Saplings was listed from the first minute.** It's a caravan find, meant to be a surprise when the fourth caravan comes back. Nothing in that list checked. Now a find isn't listed until the caravans have brought it.
- **Every building without extra storage said it was "a drop-off point where you train",** including the Workshop, the Log Rack and Oak Saplings. Only the Training Cache is. Each building now says what it's for in its own words (its `benefit` in the data), or what it does: extra storage, room for logs set aside for you, or trees planted. Anything else just shows its cost.
