## Gameplay

- **Training → Supply Workshop** delivers one load of needed raw ingredients, using your bag first or collecting from Main Storage. Choose another bench in Training to supply it.
- **Projects → Help deliver** collects one load of the project's reserved stock from Main Storage, or uses materials already in your bag. Clicking an unfinished site uses the same action while it needs materials.
- **Projects → Help build** starts hands-on work once all materials arrive. You can also click the supplied site. A builder can work alongside you. Moving or pressing X stops your work, and you can come back to the progress you left.
- **Soul Separation** gives a specialist your chosen XP at a fixed level. The preview shows the new spirit's role and what you lose. Haulers use the established routes for stores, stations, and training support. Builders supply and work on assigned projects.

Hauling starts at six units per trip and adds two per level up to the size of your bag. A player delivery earns eight XP per unit the destination accepts. Station deliveries are limited to raw inputs that cannot be routed back as production outputs. Project deliveries are limited to remaining material needs. Walking, fetching, ordinary transfers, and automated delivery grant no player XP.

Construction earns three XP per tick of personal work at a fully supplied site. Each level applies the same build-time reduction for players and spirits. Workers contribute to the same progress, with their contribution scaled to their own build time. The Hearth of Spirits requires level 3 on new projects. Existing projects retain their original requirements.

Pioneer Workforce starts with the Workshop and Main Storage, zero XP, empty stores, and no spirits. In the development profile, three Workshop loads earn the first separation. Choose the Starting Grove as a training area, then build the Training Cache as your first manual project. Turn the earned Construction XP into a builder and use that builder for a Log Rack or Storehouse while you return to training. The normal opening retains both founding helpers.

## Accounting and saves

Save format 9 remains in use. The new task variants round-trip with the actor's queue. Each project tracks its actual share of the reserved stock at Main Storage. Project pickup and competing deliveries cannot consume someone else's reservation. In-progress older projects reconstruct their holds from their recorded claims, delivered quantities, and assigned worker's cargo. Interrupted loads stay physically in the bag. The site accepts each needed unit once and completion consumes the delivered materials once.

The Still Boat returns earned specialists' gifted XP to the corresponding disciplines through the existing XP-return path. This milestone preserves that ending rule.

## Playtest

1. Start `cargo run --release -- --scenario pioneer_workforce` and gather logs in the Starting Grove.
2. Open **Training** with T. Use **Supply Workshop** three times, letting each load arrive. Open **Soul** with R, choose Hauling, and compare the smallest sacrifice with giving everything.
3. In **Training**, choose **Train at Starting Grove** and **Build Training Cache**. Use **Projects** (B) to deliver the materials and help build. Try pressing X partway through and resuming. Save during a trip and reload with F9.
4. Create a Construction Spirit from the earned XP. Make planks at the Workshop, put them in Main Storage with logs, and order a Log Rack. Watch the builder finish it while you train elsewhere.
5. Try the same actions in a normal game with the founding spirits. Note whether the Projects panel makes clear which work is yours and which work a spirit has already started.

Manual deliveries currently cover project requirements and raw station inputs. Free-form storage transfers remain available as ordinary inventory actions. A manual action schedules one delivery or one project's construction work. The scenario uses the development profile in the Bevy scenario menu. The automated runs measure progression and stability. They leave open how many loads feel repetitive in real play.

For the next milestone, play Pioneer at normal speed before adjusting XP or adding delivery destinations. Compare how often players want to help a founding worker with how often they choose to retain their own mastery.
