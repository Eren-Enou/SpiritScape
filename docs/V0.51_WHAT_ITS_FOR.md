# v0.51: What it's for

**Player problem.** In the Projects tab, where you choose what to build, each button only said what a building costs: "Plan Stone Causeway: 20 Logs, 60 Stone". What it would do for you was in the Progress tab, in a card called "Settlement improvements". That card listed every building the player could ever order, built or not, two or three lines each. In the full-map scenario it ran well past the bottom of the window, and most of it repeated what the Projects, Training and Production tabs already offer.

So the one thing you need when deciding, what it's for, was in the wrong tab, buried in a list too long to read.

**Now each tab says what it should.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Projects tab: a new card, "What they're for".** One line for each building you can plan now, in the same order as the buttons above it: "Stone Causeway: A way over the river straight to the Distant Forest, without going round by the bridge." The words are each building's own `benefit` from the data, which the plan list already required.
- **Progress tab: "Settlement improvements" is now "What you've built".** It shows Main Storage and only what's standing, each with what it's for. What could be built is in the tab where it's ordered.
- **One description for each building,** used in both places, so the two can't disagree.

### What I decided

- **A separate card below the buttons, not longer buttons.** Buttons with two lines of text are harder to scan, and the costs are what you compare at a glance.
- **Built only, in Progress.** It's the record of what you've done. The list of what you could do lives where you'd do it.

**What would count as failure:** a plannable building with no description, or a built one missing from "What you've built".

## Measured

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| The Progress card, full-map scenario | every building the player could order, two lines each, past the bottom of the window | 9 buildings, 16 lines, all on screen |
| Plan buttons that say what a building does | none | all, in the card below |

The reference game doesn't read the panel: all 19 goals by 16:57:21, and two runs are identical.

- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean.
- **All 44 UI scripts pass.** I inspected the Projects and Progress tabs in the project-queue and full-map scenarios.

The script now expects the card's new title, "WHAT YOU'VE BUILT". Two v0.50 unit tests about the old card were rewritten for the new one in the same way.
- The save format is unchanged.
