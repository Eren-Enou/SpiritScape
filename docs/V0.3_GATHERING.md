# v0.3: Sustainable gathering and skill progression

This milestone tests one question: *can sacrificing personal skill progress
create a satisfying permanent benefit, while the resulting workforce creates
interesting resource-management decisions?*

## What already existed

Before this milestone, the game already had:
- tree depletion and regrowth;
- spirits claiming a tree so they don't share it;
- spirits picking the next available tree, and going idle when the stock
  target is met;
- exact XP transfer and the resource ledger;
- deterministic ticks;
- the field-storage planner.

None of these were rebuilt. Everything below extends them.

## Systems added or changed

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Tree types (`defs.toml`) | `level_required` per tree type. **Tree** (level 1) and **Oak** (level 5) differ in XP, logs per tree, chop time and regrowth. |
| Skill rules | The player (when clicking or chopping) and spirits (when choosing a tree) obey the same requirement. If a chop becomes illegal, it stops. |
| Soul Separation | `World::soul_preview` reports your current and resulting level, the spirit's permanent level, which trees you lose and which the spirit can cut. The UI shows this before you press the button, and a banner appears afterwards. |
| Skill history | Per skill: highest level ever reached, total XP sacrificed, and spirits created. Level-ups announce unlocks ("You can now cut Oak") and regained levels. |
| Gathering sites | `[[sites]]` are named map areas; every tree inside belongs to that site. A per-site `regrow_pct` scales regrowth. Each site can be closed to spirits, reserving it for your own training (Gathering tab, or `Command::SetSiteOpen`). |
| Spirit behavior | A gatherer stays at its current site while that site has a free tree it can cut. It moves to another site only if that site has at least a full load of free logs; otherwise it waits for regrowth. Waits record a reason (recovery, storage or other), and moves are logged with a reason. |
| Measurements (`metrics.rs`) | Per spirit: time chopping, walking, handling, building, waiting for regrowth, blocked by storage, and idle (two-minute rolling window). Per site: measured logs/min, estimated sustainable logs/min, trees and logs available, and workers. Plus an estimate of the gatherers' total chop capacity and a **bottleneck** with a plain-language summary. |
| UI | **Map:** oaks have golden crowns; nearby trees you're too low level for get a red ring; each spirit has a coloured status ring and label (green chopping, blue walking, teal carrying, orange waiting for trees, grey idle, red blocked); each site has a banner with a trees-standing bar and a note when it's reserved or spirits are waiting; hovering a tree or building describes it (tier, level needed, logs, regrowth time). **Panel:** cards with bars and chips: your level and XP bar, which tiers you can cut, the Soul Separation preview, stock against the target, spirit rows with status dots, and (Gathering tab, `G`) the bottleneck, per-site bars and stacked time-use bars. The panel scrolls with the mouse wheel. **Event log:** colour-coded by source; routine spirit messages (starting a job, going idle, waiting for regrowth) are hidden unless you switch to "all events". |
| Scenarios | New: `regrind`, `grove_sustainable`, `grove_oversubscribed`. The demo now reserves the Starting Grove for the player, so its woodcutter goes straight to the forest. The field-storage sequence is unchanged: decision at 2:35, built at 3:30. |
| Planner | `min_leg_tiles` rose from 12 to 16. At 12, it built a pointless field storage seven tiles from Main Storage for spirits working the town grove. |

## How tiers and recovery work

| | Tree | Oak |
|---|---|---|
| Level required | 1 | 5 |
| XP per log | 15 | 30 |
| Logs per tree | 3 | 5 |
| Chop time (level 1) | 2.4 s (-0.08 s per level) | 3.0 s (-0.1 s per level) |
| Regrowth (base) | 60 s | 240 s |

A tree can be chopped until its logs run out. It then becomes a stump and regrows
completely after its regrowth time. There's no permanent depletion.

| Site | Trees | Regrowth scale | Max logs | Sustainable (estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starting Grove (next to town) | 8 Trees + 3 Oaks | 100% | 39 | ~28 logs/min |
| Distant Forest (across the river) | 22 Oaks | 75% (oaks regrow in 180 s) | 110 | ~37 logs/min |

"Sustainable" is the sum, over a site's trees, of logs per tree divided by
regrowth time. It's labelled as an estimate everywhere, because real
recovery is slightly slower: a tree only starts regrowing once it is fully
cut down. The UI shows it next to the **measured** rate (logs actually cut in
the last 120 s) and the gatherers' **chop capacity** (what they could cut if
they did nothing but chop).

## How Soul Separation affects retraining

- XP moves exactly. A spirit's level is the level of the XP it received.
- To make a spirit that can cut Oak, you must give it at least 388 XP (level 5).
- You lose Oak whenever your remaining XP drops below 388. Spirits never earn XP. No XP is refunded and there are no catch-up bonuses.
- Lifetime history (highest level, XP sacrificed, spirits created) is kept separately, so the sacrifice stays visible after you retrain.

### Observed in the scripted regrind run

Run it with:

```bash
cargo run -p spirit_sim --example regrind_playtest -- 400
```

| Sacrifice | Player level | Spirit level | Time to retrain to Oak | Spirit delivered meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 400 XP | 6 -> 2 | 5 | 1.3 min | 10 logs (stock target 60 then reached) |
| 500 XP | 6 -> 1 | 5 | 1.5 min | 20 logs |
| 700 XP | 7 -> 1 | 7 | 1.8 min | 20 logs |

During retraining the grove ran at its regrowth limit (~28 logs/min), because
the player and the new spirit were cutting the same trees.

## How to test sustainable versus excessive harvesting

| Scenario | Command | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Sustainable | `cargo run --release -- --scenario grove_sustainable --gathering` | One level 5 spirit; the grove never runs out; no spirit turns orange ("waiting for trees"). |
| Oversubscribed | `cargo run --release -- --scenario grove_oversubscribed --gathering` | Four spirits; the grove is empty within about 1:30, spirits turn orange ("waiting for trees"), the grove banner's bar goes red, and the bottleneck is "Resource recovery". |
| Both sites open | Oversubscribed, then click **Allow spirits** under Distant Forest in the Gathering tab | Spirits move to the forest when the grove runs dry, and the planner builds a field storage there. |
| Reserving a site | Any scenario: click **Reserve for me** under Starting Grove in the Gathering tab | Spirits finish their current tree and leave; you can still chop there. |
| Regrind | `cargo run --release -- --scenario regrind` | Chop two Trees to unlock Oak, cut Oak, then sacrifice 400+ XP (`+100` buttons) and read the preview first. |

Without graphics, the same runs print a report every minute:

```bash
QUIET=1 cargo run -p spirit_sim --example headless_demo -- 600 grove_oversubscribed
```

## Observed production rates and bottlenecks

All runs are deterministic, so these numbers repeat exactly. "Measured" is the
last two minutes; walking and waiting are shares of working time.

| Scenario, time | Measured | Sustainable | Chop / walk / wait | Bottleneck |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grove sustainable, 2-10 min | 18.5-20 logs/min | 27.8 | 68-74% / 25-31% / 0% | Gathering speed |
| Grove oversubscribed, 1 min | 39 (draining stock) | 27.8 | 29% / 16% / 53% | Resource recovery |
| Grove oversubscribed, 3-5 min | 24 | 27.8 | 20% / 9-10% / 67-69% | Resource recovery |
| Demo, 1-4 min (no field storage) | 10-13 | 36.7 (forest) | 41-46% / 53-57% / 0% | Travel distance |
| Demo, 6-8 min (field storage) | 23-24 | 36.7 (forest) | 80-82% / 17-19% / 0% | Gathering speed |
| Oversubscribed, both sites open, 3 min | 49 (forest, draining) | 36.7 | 53% / 46% / 0% | Travel distance |

Takeaways:
- In the oversubscribed grove, four spirits could cut about 114 logs/min but get about 24: regrowth is the limit, and the UI says so.
- In the demo, the field storage roughly doubles measured output (from about 12 to about 23 logs/min), and the bottleneck moves from travel to chopping speed.
- With both sites open, each of the four spirits changed site at most three times in ten minutes, each time because its site had truly run out. Every move is logged with its reason.

## Automated tests

`cargo test -p spirit_sim` runs 40 tests, all passing:
- the 22 existing simulation tests (3 adapted to the new tiers);
- 5 unit tests;
- 13 new tests in `crates/spirit_sim/tests/gathering.rs`.

The new tests cover:
- level requirements for the player and for spirits;
- the preview, including invalid amounts;
- losing access after a sacrifice, including mid-chop;
- the spirit keeping its permanent competency;
- retraining to regain access, with lifetime history kept;
- exact XP accounting;
- site membership and regrowth scaling;
- a site recovering fully after exhaustion;
- sustainable output with one spirit;
- shortages with four spirits;
- waiting and resuming;
- relocating without oscillating;
- reserving a site without creating resources;
- conservation and replay in every scenario.

The demo's field-storage test still passes, and the event order is unchanged.

## What was and wasn't verified

- **Verified headlessly:** all numbers above, and every acceptance criterion except "feels significant".
- **Verified in screenshots of the running game:**
  - the Gathering tab during the oversubscribed run;
  - the separation banner and history (demo with `--separate 1200`);
  - the two tree tiers, site outlines and labels.
- **Not verified by hand:** clicking the new buttons (site toggles, tab switch, scenario buttons). Also not tried: reading the preview mid-game, or the regrind loop played with real input.

## Design questions for playtesting

1. **Is the sacrifice too cheap?** Retraining back to Oak takes 1.3-1.8 minutes, which may make losing a level feel trivial. Things to tune:
   - the Oak level requirement (5);
   - the XP table;
   - the Tree vs Oak XP gap (15 vs 30);
   - a third tier.
2. **Should spirits compete with the player for the same grove?** During retraining the grove is at its limit. Do players reach for "reserve for you", or does it feel like the spirit is stealing their trees?
3. **Is the bottleneck readout understandable without the numbers?** For example, does "Workers could gather faster, but trees are recovering" lead players to open the forest or build more spirits?
4. **Is a spirit level shown as a number enough?** A level 5 and a level 10 Oak spirit feel similar on screen. Should the spirit's competency be more visible, for example chop speed shown in its label?
5. **Should a sacrifice that loses access ask for confirmation?** The preview is always shown, but the button acts immediately.
6. **What should a spirit do while waiting?** Today it stands still where it is. Should it gather lower-tier trees, help hauling, or rest at storage?
7. **Should spirits be able to cut lower tiers at all?** An Oak spirit currently also cuts Trees, which is what makes it compete with a retraining player.
