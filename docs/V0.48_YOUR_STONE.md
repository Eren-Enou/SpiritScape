# v0.48: Your stone

**Player problem.** The old stones are the game's story: someone who gave everything to their helpers, built no hearth, and left a line on a stone at each step. Both endings answer them. "Whoever cut those stones built no hearth. You did." But the answer is the game's, not yours, and it's a card that's gone when you close it. Nothing on the map says you were here.

**Now, once either ending has happened, you can carve a stone of your own, where you stand.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

### What it says

Most of it is what you did, worked out when you carve it:

- After the Hearth: "I gave 1542 XP of myself to make 2 helpers. We built a hearth, and they stood by it."
- After the Still Boat: "I gave 1542 XP of myself to make 2 helpers. Then I called them home, and took it back."
- If two spirits kept each other company: "Oakroot and Fernlight kept each other company. I saw it."
- If you read every old stone: "I read every stone the last one left. This one is for whoever comes next."

The last line is yours. You choose one of three:

- "Whoever finds this: give some of it away. Not all of it."
- "Whoever finds this: they look up, if you let them."
- "Whoever finds this: I'm still here."

Each answers the old stones in a different way: as advice, as what you saw in your spirits, or as the thing the old carver lost.

### Where and when

- **Only once the game is over,** by either ending. While spirits are still boarding the boat, it isn't over yet.
- **Where you stand.** Walk somewhere first if you want it by the Hearth, or on the shore, or by the first old stone. The Progress tab says so.
- **Once.** A stone is a stone.
- **The words don't change afterwards,** like the old stones. If you rename a spirit later, the stone still has the old name.

### What remembers it

- It stands on the map: pale and new, a little shorter than the old ones.
- Both endings' closing words add: "You left a stone of your own, for whoever comes next."
- The notes (`L`) get a section, "Your stone".

### What I decided

- **The choice of last line changes nothing else.** It's the one thing in the game the player says in their own voice, and making it pay out would make it a choice about the payout.
- **It isn't a goal, and there's no tip.** It's offered in the Progress tab after the ending, which is where the ending's story already is.
- **The rules and every line are in `data/defs.toml`, under `[your_stone]`.**

**What would count as failure:** carving before the end, carving twice, a stone that says something the game didn't do, or a stone missing after loading a save.

## Measured

The reference game never ends a game by choice, so nothing it measures changes: all 19 goals by 16:57:21, and two runs are identical.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **651 pass**, 644 from before plus 7 new.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean.
- **All 43 UI scripts pass,** including the new `ui_your_stone.txt`, which carves a stone in the Midwinter scenario. I inspected its screenshots.
- The save format stays at 9. The new field defaults to empty, and a test checks the stone survives a save.

## Not verified

- **Whether the words land,** or whether three choices is too few, or too many.
- Whether anyone carves one at all, after the ending card.
- Whether the stone is easy to see on a busy map.
