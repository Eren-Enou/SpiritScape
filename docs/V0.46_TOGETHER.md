# v0.46: Together

**Player problem.** Spirits had small moments of their own, but they never seemed to know each other was there. The Hearth also stopped mattering once it was lit: spirits gathered at it once, and then it was a building.

**Now spirits notice each other, and every winter they go back to the Hearth.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

### Spirits who notice each other

Six new moments need another spirit within 8 tiles, and name them:

- "Oakroot waited for Mossbell at the bend in the path."
- "Oakroot left something on Mossbell's pile, and didn't mention it."
- "Oakroot and Mossbell have started keeping the same hours." (after 6 hours)

They go in Their Story with the other spirit's name, as it was at the time. Like every moment, they change nothing in the world.

### Midwinter

Once the Hearth is lit, every spirit stops at the start of each winter and goes to stand by it, for 90 seconds. Then they go back to work.

It's the same gathering as the night it was lit, so the Hearth stays part of their year. It costs a little work, which seemed right: it should be worth something to them.

### A midwinter scenario

Midwinter only happens in a real game after the Hearth, so no check could reach it, and I hadn't seen it on screen. There's now a `midwinter` scenario: the Hearth is lit and it's the last minute of autumn. Scenarios normally have no seasons, so this one turns them on, and a test checks that no other scenario has them.

### The years

Each spring, your notes get a line for the year that ended: "Year 3: 4210 gathered, 980 made, 1 new spirit, 4 things built." A long game needs some way to see how far it's come.

### A nudge about names

Naming a spirit does something now, so there's a tip saying you can.

**What would count as failure:** a placeholder showing in the text, a spirit stuck at the Hearth, or midwinter costing real progress.

## What seeing it found

The first look at midwinter on screen, in the new scenario, found three things:

- **Seasons were switched off in every scenario,** because scenarios run at the fast development pace, and the season code ignores that pace. A scenario that asks for seasons now gets them. A test checks that the others still don't.
- **Over their heads, the spirits at the Hearth said "idle"** while the panel said "At the Hearth". Idle reads as nothing to do, which is the opposite of what's happening. They now say "at the Hearth" there too, with a test.
- **The three Hearth stages' labels ran into each other** when the stages stood on neighbouring tiles. I spaced them out in the scenario. A real game could place them side by side too, so that's worth a look in a playtest.

## Measured

- **All 19 goals by 16:57:21,** so midwinter costs the reference game 4 seconds. Only one winter comes after its Hearth.
- **101 moments in that game,** up from 91.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **635 pass**, 621 from before plus 14 new, including one that checks spirits really do walk to the Hearth and not just that the flag is set.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean.
- **Forty-one UI scripts pass,** including the new `ui_midwinter.txt`.
- **`ui_moments2.txt` now expects a different moment.** The new pair moments joined the pool, so Mossbell's first moment in the quiet hour is now "sharpened an axe the way you used to" instead of "just to see". The script still checks that a moment reaches a spirit's story.
- The save format stays at 9, because the new fields default to empty.
- CI runs fmt, Clippy, the tests and the long runs on the PR.

## Not verified

- Whether spirits noticing each other makes a difference to anyone.
- Whether the Hearth stages' labels overlap in a real game.
- A spirit's name in an old moment stays as it was if the other spirit is renamed later. That felt right, but it's a choice.
