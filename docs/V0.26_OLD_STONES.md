# v0.26: The Old Stones

**Player problem.** There was nothing to discover. Every place on the map was a resource, and nothing gave the world a past or the Hearth a reason.

**The map now has a story to find.** Six stones stand around the map, cut by someone who was here before you. They gave everything away to their helpers, a piece at a time, until nobody was left. It's the game's central trade, followed to its end. Nobody has read them yet, so whether they land is unknown.

## Design

- **Stones are data:** `[[markers]]` in `defs.toml`, each with a place, a title and text.
- **Reading one:** walk up to it. Only the player reads them, once each.
- **Any order:** each stands on its own. The last is beyond the ravine, so it takes the Forest Crossing.
- **The Old Stones:** a card in the Progress tab (`J`) keeps what you've read.
- **The ending changes:** if you've read all six, Your Story adds a line that answers them.
- **New goal:** Read the old stones, making 17.

They change nothing about how the game plays. There's no reward, because the point is the reading.

**What would count as failure:** a stone that can't be reached, or one read from far away.

## Measured

The reference player reads all six in under a minute of walking, at the end of its long game. All 17 goals are met by 13:11:57.

## Not verified

- Physical mouse and keyboard.
- **Whether anyone notices the stones, or reads them.**
- Whether a stone is noticeable at play distance. My first version used a rock model and vanished among the map's other rocks in the overview, so they're now tall, pale and faintly glowing.
- Whether the text is any good. It's mine, and it needs a reader.
