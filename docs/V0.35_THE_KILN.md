# v0.35: The Kiln

**Player problem.** After the Hearth there was no new trade to learn, and winter only ever took something away. A Quarrying Spirit also sat idle unless a project asked for Stone, so you couldn't build up a stock ahead of time.

**There's now a fourth trade, a way out of winter, and a way to keep raw materials in stock.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

### Kilnwork

The seventh Trade Caravan brings back a lump of river clay.

| Step | What it takes |
|---|---|
| Clay Pits | Plants six clay banks along the near river |
| Kiln | A new bench, after the Clay Pits |
| Charcoal | 3 Logs, from Kilnwork level 1 |
| Bricks | 2 Clay and 1 Charcoal make 2, from level 5 |

That's the longest chain yet: logs to charcoal, then charcoal and clay to bricks. Clay is dug by Quarrying Spirits of level 3 or more, and it freezes in winter like the trees.

### What Bricks are for

- **Warm Walls:** brick walls with a flue through them. Trees and clay keep recovering through winter at half speed.
- **Hearth Chimney:** does nothing except add a line to the ending. It's something to build after the Hearth, and it isn't meant to be useful.
- **A bridge-builder** comes to buy them.

### Keeping stock

The Gathering tab now has a Keep in Stock card for Stone and Clay. Spirits that gather them keep that much in Main Storage, with nothing asking. It stops at what Main Storage can hold.

### The ending now reads from data

Any building can have an `ending_line`. The Still Lantern's line moved there, and the chimney uses it too, so later milestones can add more.

**What would count as failure:** clay being unreachable, a Quarrying Spirit digging past its target, or an old save losing its orders.

## Bugs this found

- **A crash on launch.** A list of order names was still four long when the buttons went to six, and only the UI scripts caught it. Those lists now take their length from the button count, so it can't compile wrong again.
- **Too many orders on screen.** Two more orders pushed the Production tab past the bottom of the window, and a UI script caught it. Orders for a bench you haven't built no longer show at all, which tidies that tab from the start of the game.
- **A stalled reference game.** I first had the reference player send caravans whenever a goal needed one. It then reached the Hearth with no Masonry Spirit, and sat there for 60 hours. Late caravans now wait for the late game. A person can cancel a plan, so this doesn't trap anyone.

## Not verified

- Whether the Kiln looks like a kiln. It uses the same wooden parts as other buildings, because there's no brick model.
- **Whether a chain this long is interesting, or just more waiting.**
- Whether seven caravans is too many to ask before the trade opens.
