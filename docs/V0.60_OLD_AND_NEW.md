# v0.60: Old and new

**Player problem.** In v0.56 you can ask the last of the old carver's helpers home, and in v0.57 it walks there and stands by your fire. Then nothing. Your spirits work around it as if it weren't there. The old stones' story is about helpers who "never stop" and "don't look up", and here are two kinds of helper, the old carver's and yours, side by side, with nothing between them.

**Now your spirits notice it.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

Four new small moments, for a spirit within 8 tiles of the old helper once it's home and still:

- "Oakroot stood beside the old helper for a while. Neither of them did anything."
- "Oakroot watched the old helper's hands, which were still for once."
- "Oakroot brought the old helper a log, then took it back to the pile."
- "Oakroot and the old helper looked up at the same time."

The last one answers the Mossed Stone: "The helpers never stop. They cut and carry and build, and they don't look up."

- **Like every moment, they change nothing in the world.** The log goes back to the pile.
- **They need the helper home and still:** not while it walks, and never if it was left by its stone.
- **They're new definitions at the end of the list,** with a new condition, `near_helper`, so nothing else's order changes.

**What would count as failure:** a moment about the helper when it isn't there.
