# v0.38: Small moments

**Player problem.** Spirits have names, traits and deeds, but between those they're just workers. The old stones warn that the helpers "don't look up". Nothing in the game ever showed yours doing anything else.

**Now they do small things, for no reason.** One spirit does something every ten game minutes. It changes nothing. Nobody has played it.

## Design

"Mossbell picked up one more than it needed to, just to see."

- **26 moments in `data/defs.toml`,** each with a condition: a trait, a season, a skill, near the southern water, or none.
- **They show over the spirit for 8 seconds,** go in the event log, and the last three stay in Their Story.
- **Some echo you.** A spirit made from your Woodcutting might sharpen an axe the way you used to. Only spirits made from your own XP do these. A spirit you never made has nothing of yours.
- **Some echo the stones.** A spirit by the water "stopped at the water, and turned round".
- **Naming matters.** A spirit you've named is three times as likely to be the one, and only named spirits have the moments about their names. It's the first thing a name actually does.
- **Nobody repeats themselves.** A spirit that has done everything it can just stays quiet, which seemed better than sounding mechanical.

### Why every ten minutes

I started at six. In a whole game they fired on only half their turns, because older spirits had run out by about hour eight. Ten keeps them rarer, and they last longer.

**What would count as failure:** a moment changing anything in the world, a spirit saying the same thing twice, or moments getting in the way of reading the map.

## Measured

- **71 moments in the 17-hour reference game,** so about one every 14 minutes.
- **By spirit:** 9, 9, 11, 12, 11, 11, 5, 3 and 0. The later numbers are spirits made late.
- **The reference game is unchanged:** all 19 goals by 17:01:27. A test also compares every stock, position and tree with moments on and off.

**The oldest spirits are close to running out** by the end of that game. A longer game will need more moments.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **562 pass**, 553 from before plus 9 new.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Thirty-five UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_moments2.txt`.
- The save format stays at 9, because the new fields default to empty.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- **Whether this makes anyone care.** That's the whole point of it, and no test can show it.
- Whether the words read as charming or twee.
- Whether a moment is easy to miss, or distracting, at normal play speed.

## Human playtest

```bash
cargo run --release -- --scenario quiet_hour
```

Watch for ten minutes, or use `8x`. Then rename a spirit and carry on.
