# v0.58: Two games in a row

**Player problem.** v0.49 keeps the stone you carve and stands it in your next game. Every piece was tested on its own: keeping the words, reading them back, a new game standing them up, and walking up to one. But nobody had played one real game to its end and then another, and the v0.49 doc said so. A story that only works in pieces isn't checked.

**Now a long test plays it as one story, and it found something.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

### The test

`tests/two_games.rs`, release only, like the other long runs:

1. The reference player plays a whole real game to the Hearth, meeting every goal.
2. It carves a stone, choosing "I'm still here". The test checks the words are this game's: "I gave ... XP of myself ... We built a hearth".
3. A new real game is given that stone, as the game window gives it what `stones.txt` kept.
4. The player walks up to the stone, and finds it, with its words exactly as carved, in the event log and the notes.

A second test checks that the reference game's old-stones story hangs together at its end, with no placeholder left in the closing words.

Both run on CI now, in the long-runs job, and in my local check.

### What it found

The reference player reads all seven old stones, the Last Stone included. **It never met the last helper.** It read the stone from its far side, two tiles from the helper, and walked on. A person could do exactly the same, and the end of the old stones' story, the one thing v0.56 added, would be lost.

So:

- **The helper stands one tile closer to its stone,** at (57, 10).
- **It looks up when you come within three tiles,** set in the data as `notices_within = 3`. From every side the Last Stone can be read from, that's close enough.
- A new test stands a fresh game's player on each side of the Last Stone and checks that reading it is enough to meet the helper.

The question still waits as long as you like. It just can't be walked past.

**What would count as failure:** a stone from one game not found in the next, or the Last Stone read and the helper never met.
