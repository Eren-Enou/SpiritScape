# v0.28: Visitors

**Player problem.** Nothing ever happened to the settlement. Everything was the player's own plan, and no moment asked for a decision.

**Now people pass through.** Five visitors come during a real game, each with an offer to take or turn down before they leave. Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Data:** `[[visitors]]`, with who they are, what they say, what they want and what they give.
- **In order, once each,** after a set time, and some only once a building stands: the surveyor needs an outpost and the old mason needs a yard.
- **Ten game minutes to answer,** then they move on.
- **Real goods:** an offer moves stock in and out of Main Storage, and the ledger balances.
- **Every offer costs something.** It has to be possible to reasonably say no.
- **On the Road:** a card below What's Next, in real games only.

| Visitor | Wants | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| A pedlar | 40 Logs | 15 Planks |
| A surveyor | 40 Planks | 30 Stone |
| A shipwright | 20 Beams | 40 Panels |
| An old mason | 20 Dressed Stone, 10 Beams | 8 Keystones |
| A pilgrim | 30 Panels | 1 Keystone |

The pilgrim is a poor deal on purpose. They've walked a long way to see the stones.

### Rejected

- **Random arrivals:** the simulation has no randomness, and a fixed order keeps every run repeatable.
- **Threats or losses:** a visitor taking something from you would punish absent players, and this game lets you walk away.

**What would count as failure:** an offer that's always right, or goods appearing or vanishing unaccounted for.

## Measured

The reference players ignore visitors, so no long-game number changes. I haven't measured whether any offer is well priced. That's a guess until someone plays.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **494 pass**, 483 from before plus 11 in `tests/visitors.rs`. No existing assertion changed.
- Six release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Twenty-seven UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_visitors.txt`. It only covers the empty card, because the first visitor takes 25 game minutes to arrive. Accepting and declining are tested in the simulation.
- The save format stays at 8. New fields have defaults, and a visitor who's waiting survives saving.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- Physical mouse and keyboard.
- The offer buttons on screen, with a visitor present.
- **Whether a visitor is a welcome interruption, or easy to miss.**
