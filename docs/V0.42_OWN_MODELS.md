# v0.42: Our own models

**Player problem.** The Kiln, Warm Walls, Hearth Chimney and Still Boat were all stand-ins made of the same wooden parts, and the clay banks were recoloured rocks. Two of those are the most important things in the late game, and a player couldn't have told what they were.

**They now have models of their own.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

`tools/make_models.py` builds them from nothing but Python, the same way `tools/make_sounds.py` makes the sounds. It writes binary glTF into `assets/models/own/`.

- **Kiln:** a squat brick dome with a flue, a glowing firebox door and fuel by the door.
- **Warm Walls:** an L of low brick wall, with a flue at the corner and a small fire at its foot.
- **Hearth Chimney:** a tall stack, stepped in as it rises.
- **Still Boat:** a pale narrow hull, tarred below the rail, with a lantern on a post at the bow.
- **Clay banks:** a low wet mound, and a dark scrape once it's been dug.

They're flat blocks of colour, to sit alongside the rest of the world. They're original, and need no licence file.

### Why generate them

There's no artist, and I can't draw in a modelling program here. Doing it in code means the models are small, can be regenerated and changed in a text editor, and there's no question about where they came from.

**What would count as failure:** a model that doesn't load, an inside-out surface, or something that looks out of place next to the rest.

## Measured

- **About 240 KB in all.** The chimney is the biggest, at 140 KB, because each brick is its own block.
- **The reference game is unchanged,** because nothing here touches the simulation.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **598 pass**, 597 from before plus 1 new, which checks the game uses its own models. The test that every model named in the visuals file exists came with the release candidate.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Thirty-seven UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_models.txt`, with a new `brickyard` scenario that puts them side by side.
- The save format stays at 9.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- I've looked at these in screenshots, at one camera angle. Nobody has walked around them.
- The boat is at the edge of my screenshot, so I've seen less of it than the others.
- Frame rate with the chimney built. It's the only model with hundreds of parts, though only one can exist.
