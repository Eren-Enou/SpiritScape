# v0.57: The walk home

**Player problem.** In v0.56, when you asked the last helper home, the words said "It puts the chisel down and follows you home." Then it was simply at your fire, the moment you answered, from the far side of the map. The one thing the words promised was the one thing that didn't happen.

**Now it walks.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Asked home, it walks from its stone to beside your fire,** along a real path through the map, over the Forest Crossing and round the water.
- **It walks at half your pace.** It's old. In the Last Stone scenario the walk is about 55 tiles and takes about 43 seconds.
- **It stops a step or two from the Hearth if it's lit, or from Main Storage,** facing the way it came.
- **It plays its walking animation on the way** and turns to face where it's going, as spirits do.
- **If there's no way through,** for example if the crossing has gone, it's simply at your fire, as it was before.
- **A walk saved halfway carries on** where it was.

**What would count as failure:** it walking through water, stopping short, or vanishing on a reload.

## Measured

The reference game never meets it, so nothing it measures changes: all 19 goals by 16:57:21, and two runs are identical.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **686 pass**, 685 from before plus 1 new.
- **One changed assertion:** v0.56's `asked_home_it_stands_by_the_fire_and_does_no_work` checked that the helper was beside Main Storage the moment it was asked. Now it starts at its stone and walks, so the test checks that instead. A new test follows the walk: three tiles in three tile-times, ending within two tiles of Main Storage, and the same after a save halfway.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean.
- **All 45 UI scripts pass.** `ui_last_helper.txt` now runs on at 8x after the answer and takes a last screenshot. It shows the helper by Main Storage across the map. I inspected it, and one taken just after the answer, with the helper setting off from its stone.
- The save format stays at 9. The walk is a new field that defaults to empty, so a v0.56 save with the helper already home loads with it at the fire.
