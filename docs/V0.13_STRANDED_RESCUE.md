# v0.13: Nobody Stays Walled In

**Player problem.** v0.9 stopped new buildings from walling anyone in, but a Construction Spirit already shut in by an older save stayed there for good. With the only builder trapped, no project could ever finish, and nothing told the player why.

**This is fixed, and it covers more than the one case.** Every 10 seconds the game checks that everyone is on the main stretch of open ground. Anyone who isn't steps out to the nearest edge of it.

## Design

- **Mainland:** the largest connected area of open ground. Being next to Main Storage doesn't count, because the pocket that trapped the builder was.
- **The rescue:** the spirit or player moves to the nearest tile of that ground, and a notice says so. It's a short jump, not a walk, because there's no route.
- **What's kept:** their tasks, bag and any reservation. A walk just finds its route again, so nothing is lost and the ledger isn't touched.
- **Runs all the time, not only on loading:** this catches any way of being cut off, including ones I haven't thought of.
- **Hollow Grove:** while it's shut it isn't the mainland, and nobody can be there.

### Rejected

- **Fixing it during save migration:** that handles only saves already known to be damaged, and would need a format change.
- **Removing a building to open the pocket:** destroying the player's work is a worse surprise than a spirit hopping out.

**What would count as failure:** anyone on open ground being moved, or a rescued builder still unable to finish a project.

## Measured

- **In the `walled_in` scenario,** the builder is out within 10 seconds, and a Storehouse planned at the start gets finished.
- **Nobody else moves.** Every other scenario starts with everyone on open ground, and all earlier results are unchanged: the test suite and normal routes pass as before.
- **Cost:** one scan of 1,680 tiles every 10 seconds of game time.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **387 pass**, 378 from before plus 9 in `tests/stranded.rs`. No existing assertion changed.
- Four release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Sixteen UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_walled_in.txt`.
- The save format doesn't change. A save holding a walled-in builder recovers after loading.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.
