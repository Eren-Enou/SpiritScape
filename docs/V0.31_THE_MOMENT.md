# v0.31: The Moment

**Player problem.** The ending was a card in a tab, and seasons were a word in the header. Neither could be seen in the world.

**Both now show on the map.** When the Hearth is lit, every spirit stops and comes to stand by it, the screen dims for your story, and it stays lit for the rest of the game. Winter turns the ground pale and snow falls. Nobody has seen any of this but me, in screenshots.

## Design

**The ending**

- **They come.** Each spirit finishes what it's carrying and walks to the Hearth, and they stand there for three minutes before going back to work. This is real simulation, so a saved game keeps it.
- **A closing screen** dims the world, shows your story, and waits for Enter.
- **It stays lit,** with a warm light and sparks rising from it for the rest of the game.

**The seasons**

- **A sheet of colour** over the ground fades between seasons: green in spring, orange in autumn, pale in winter. The colours are in `data/visuals.toml`.
- **Snow** falls around you in winter.

`--skip-minutes` jumps a game forward, for testing what a later season looks like.

**What would count as failure:** spirits failing to reach the Hearth or never going back to work, or carried goods being lost.

## A bug this exposed

The closing screenshot showed each spirit's label twice. On the first frame, spirits were being spawned before the world was built, and then again after, so **every spirit in every game had a duplicate.** This goes back a long way. The two copies stood on the same spot, which is why nobody saw it until they stood close together. It's fixed by ordering those two steps.

## Not verified

- Physical mouse and keyboard.
- **Whether the moment lands.** The closing text is a list of facts, and with no spirits made it reads oddly ("You gave 0 XP").
- Spring and autumn colours. I've only looked at winter.
- Trees stay green in winter, because the tint only covers the ground.
