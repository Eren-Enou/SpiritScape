# v0.39: The frozen river

**Player problem.** v0.37 measured winter at about a fifth of the reference game, and all it ever did was take something away. The Far Shore also stayed out of reach until very late.

**Now winter offers something back.** The south river freezes, and you can walk across, long before you could bridge it. Spirits won't follow. Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **The river freezes for all of winter.** The first winter starts 90 minutes in, so a new player can see the Far Shore in their first couple of hours, instead of after the South Crossing.
- **Only the player goes out on it.** To everything else it's still water, so spirits, planning and building are untouched. The old stones say "They won't cross", and this keeps that true.
- **Nobody walks on it who doesn't have to.** Your path only uses the ice if there's no way round, so clicking a tree across the ravine can't send you onto it by accident.
- **It won't last.** Three minutes before spring, if you're out there, the ice warns you once.
- **If it catches you,** you get home, but whatever you were carrying doesn't.
- **A crossing changes that.** With the South Crossing built, you can stay as long as you like.
- **It doesn't open anything new to spirits.** The Far Shore still has no spirit work, and Stillwood still needs Woodcutting 12.

### What's it for?

You can read the Shore Stone early, and see the Stilltrees you can't yet cut. Later on you can bring Stillwood home before you've built a crossing, at some risk. It's mostly there to make winter a time when you might go somewhere.

**What would count as failure:** a spirit on the ice, the player losing goods with no warning, or getting stranded for good.

## Bugs this found

**The rescue for walled-in players carried you straight home.** My tests stopped the moment you arrived, so all 13 passed. Only the screenshot showed "You climbed out of a walled-in corner" as soon as you reached the far side. The rescue now leaves you alone while the river is frozen, and new tests stay out there for five minutes.

## Measured

- **The reference game is unchanged,** because it never needs the ice: all 19 goals by 17:01:27.
- The Far Shore used to need the South Crossing, at about 16 hours in that game. It's now reachable from 1:30.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **579 pass**, 562 from before plus 17 new.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Thirty-six UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_ice.txt`, which walks across.
- The save format stays at 9, because the new fields default to empty. A test saves and loads in the middle of the ice.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- **Whether anyone tries it.** There's one tip, and the river doesn't look like an invitation.
- Whether losing your load feels fair, or just annoying.
- The ice is a flat pale band, not real ice.
- Physical keyboard walking onto the ice. The tests use the same command, but nobody has pressed the keys.
