# v0.61: The cold camp

**Player problem.** The old stones are the story of someone who gave everything to their helpers, and their last line is "We built no hearth. Nobody was left who thought of it." The Hearth ending answers it in words: "Whoever cut those stones built no hearth. You did." But there's nowhere on the map to answer it, and nothing to do once the Hearth is lit except read a card.

**Now there's a place, and something to do there.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Near the Mossed Stone there's a ring of small stones,** a few tiles south of the Old Growth, with nothing in it.
- **Walk up and you find it:** "A ring of stones near the Mossed Stone, with no ashes in it. Somebody camped here for a long time. Nobody ever lit a fire."
- **Come back to it with your own Hearth lit, and you light it:** "You've brought the light from your hearth all this way. For the first time, there's a fire in the old camp." A small fire burns there from then on, with its own light and sparks, like the Hearth's.
- **Both endings say so:** "You carried a light from your hearth to the old camp, and lit the fire they never had."
- **The notes** get a section, "The cold camp". **The play report** says whether it was found, and when it was lit.
- **It changes nothing else.** It's a place and a choice to go there.
- **The rules and words are in `data/defs.toml`, under `[cold_camp]`.**

### What I decided

- **You don't carry anything.** Walking there with the Hearth lit is enough. A torch to carry would mean a new item and a way to lose it, for a moment that's about going, not managing.
- **No tip points to it.** The Mossed Stone is the nearest old stone, and a player who's read them all knows the line it answers.

**What would count as failure:** lighting it before there's a Hearth, finding or lighting it twice, or the fire going out on a reload.

## Also fixed

The closing words said "You gave 388 XP of yourself to make 1 spirits, and it's still here, in them", and a carved stone said "to make 1 helpers". Both found in this milestone's screenshots. v0.54's check for "1" followed by a plural only covered resource names. Now one spirit is "1 spirit ... in it", a stone says "1 helper", and a test in `words.rs` checks both, and that more than one stays plural.
