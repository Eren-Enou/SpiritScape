# v0.53: Room to read

**Player problem.** Two cards sit above every tab: Now, which says what you're doing, and You, with your skills. Late in a game you have five skills, and the You card gave each a bar and a line saying what's next: nine lines. Together they filled the top half of the panel. In the full-map scenario, the Spirits tab's own cards started below the middle of the screen, and its Instructions ran off the bottom.

Every tab paid for a card most of which you weren't using.

**Now the You card shows what you're working on, and the rest in a line.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **The skill you last earned XP in is shown in full:** level, bar, XP and what it unlocks next.
- **Every other skill you've met is a small chip with its level:** "Woodworking 10", "Quarrying 5".
- **Before you've earned any XP since starting or loading,** the skill you have most XP in is shown in full.
- **Every skill is still in full in the Progress tab,** under Your skills.
- **A new game looks as it did,** because it has one skill.

### What I decided

- **The game window remembers which skill you last used, not the simulation.** It's about what you're looking at, nothing else depends on it, and it shouldn't be saved or replayed.
- **"Last earned XP" rather than "highest level".** The skill you're training is the one whose next unlock you care about.

**What would count as failure:** the card flicking between skills while you work, or a skill disappearing from it.

## Measured

| Full-map scenario | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Lines in the You card | 9 | 3 |
| Where the Spirits tab's first card starts | 623 px down | 558 px down |
| Spirits tab, Instructions | runs off the bottom | all on screen |

The reference game doesn't read the panel: all 19 goals by 16:57:21, and two runs are identical.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **674 pass**, 671 from before plus 3 new.
- Eight release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean.
- **All 44 UI scripts pass.** The four scripts that read skill text from the panel (workshop, masonry, quarry and smoke) pass unchanged. I inspected the Spirits tab in the full-map scenario before and after.
- The save format is unchanged.

## Not verified

- Whether the card switching skills when you change what you're doing is noticeable or distracting.
