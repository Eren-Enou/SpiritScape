# v0.24: Masonry

**Player problem.** Wood and stone were separate. Nothing needed both, and the third caravan had nothing to bring back.

**Masonry joins them.** It's the third trade, opened by the third Trade Caravan, and Keystones are the first goods that need both chains. All 15 goals can still be met in one game. Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Mason's Yard:** a new station, opened by the third caravan's tools. It costs 30 Stone, 120 Planks and 24 Beams in a normal game.
- **Masonry:** a trainable skill with Soul Separation and a Masonry Spirit.
- **Dressed Stone:** 2 Stone make 1, from level 1.
- **Keystones:** 2 Dressed Stone and 1 Structural Beam make 1, at level 8. It's the first recipe with two kinds of input.
- **Orders:** both goods have production orders, with buttons that appear once there's a yard, and demand runs down the chain: ask for Keystones and Dressed Stone gets made, then Stone gets cut.
- **New goal:** Cut 10 Keystones.

Keystones have no use yet. They're for the final project.

### A bug this exposed

`recipe_for` looked at the highest-level crafter of any skill, so a level 10 woodworker counted as staff for a Mason's Yard. It now checks the worker can make that product.

## Measured

Reproduce with `cargo run -p spirit_sim --release --example all_goals_report`.

| One long game | Met |
|---|---|
| Master Woodworking | 8:25 |
| Cut 10 Keystones | 10:41 |
| Master Woodcutting | 13:04 |

All 15 goals are met, with 392 units left behind. The chain ran without any special handling: Stone to Dressed Stone to Keystones.

**A gap this showed:** nothing lets a player keep a standing stock of Stone. A Quarrying Spirit only works when something asks for Stone, so training Masonry by hand means cutting your own, which is what the reference player does.
