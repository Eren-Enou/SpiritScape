# v0.32: The Far Shore

**Player problem.** Everything in the game could eventually be handed to a spirit. There was no place left that was only yours, and the map had nothing new past about ten hours.

**South of the river there's now a place your spirits won't go.** It's the map's first new region since the ravine, and it holds the one thing in the game you have to do yourself. Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **South Crossing:** needs a Mason's Yard, and costs Dressed Stone, Keystones and beams, so Keystones have a second use.
- **The Far Shore:** a new region of 12 more map rows, with ten Stilltrees and a seventh old stone.
- **Stilltrees:** Woodcutting 12, and `spirits_avoid`: no spirit will cut one. They're left out of Soul Separation's options for the same reason.
- **Still Lantern:** 30 Stillwood and 10 Dressed Stone. Every spirit carries two more, and the ending adds a line if you built it.
- **The seventh stone** explains why they stop at the water, or at least what the last person thought.
- **New goal:** Light a Still Lantern, making 18.

It fits the game's central trade: everything else you've given away, and this you have to go and do alone.

**What would count as failure:** spirits working there, or older saves losing anything when the map grows.

## Measured

Reproduce with `cargo run -p spirit_sim --release --example all_goals_report`.

| One long game | Met |
|---|---|
| Light the Hearth of Spirits | 14:10 |
| Master Woodcutting | 16:27 |
| Light a Still Lantern | 16:38 |
| Every goal | 16:49 |

The reference player does the shore last, so it's quick here. It's reached after the Hearth in this run, but nothing requires that order.

Left behind rose back to 2,240 units in this run. The reference player now chops longer before its caravans go.

## A bug this exposed

Adding a higher tree tier silently moved the reference route's target from level 10 to level 12, because it asked for "the top tier". Its first run failed for that reason. Soul Separation would also have offered a "Stilltree spirit" that could never cut one. Both now skip what spirits avoid.

## Saves

- The format is now **version 9**, because the map grew. Older saves go through `migrate_map`, and nothing already on the map moves.
- **Tested on real files:** two fixtures written by the unmodified v0.31 code, plus older ones back to v0.5.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **515 pass**, 505 from before plus 10 in `tests/far_shore.rs`.
- **One count changed:** 18 goals, in one test and one UI script.
- Six release-only long-run tests pass, including every goal in one game.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- All 30 UI scripts pass, and I inspected the whole map in the overview.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- **Whether a place the spirits won't go feels special, or just slow.**
- Whether a person would find the shore without the goal pointing at it.
