# v0.43: Notes

**Player problem.** A tip is on screen for a few seconds, and this is a 20-hour game. By hour ten nobody can remember what a caravan brought back at hour three, or what they told the pedlar. Those visitors now come back and refer to it.

**There's now somewhere to look it up.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

### The notes tab

Press `L`. It opens once your first spirit exists, and it keeps three things:

- **What you've been told:** every tip so far, in the order you heard it.
- **What the caravans found:** each find, with its instructions.
- **Who has come through:** everyone, with what you said. "A pedlar: you took the offer."

A section only appears once it has something in it. It reads what the game already keeps, so it adds nothing to a save, and older saves get their notes for free.

### A tip that came from the measurements

v0.37 showed that holding more logs saves the reference game more than an hour, and the game never said so. There's now a tip in the first autumn that says to put more logs by, and where to do it.

### Older spirits grow into things

v0.38's oldest spirits were running out of moments. There are now 7 more that only come to a spirit that's been around a while, from 5 hours up to 16. The last one needs a spirit you've named. So what a spirit does changes as it gets older, and that seemed better than just writing more of the same.

### What I decided not to do

I'd planned to bring Warm Walls forward, because winter is expensive and they come so late. The timeline changed my mind. The reference game doesn't send its first caravan until 11:14, because it builds warehouses first. So "too late" is partly about how that player behaves. Moving them on that evidence would be tuning to a bot. I've left it for a playtest.

**What would count as failure:** notes that lose something, or that reveal something the player hasn't reached.

## Measured

- **The reference game is unchanged:** all 19 goals by 17:01:27.
- **Moments in that game went from 71 to 91,** and the oldest spirits now reach 14 to 17 rather than 12.
- **34 moments and 17 tips in all.**

`all_goals_report --timeline` now prints everything new a game shows, in order, and that's what changed my mind about Warm Walls.

## A layout fix this needed

The Notes tab pushed the tab buttons onto a fourth row, which moved everything below them down. In the late-finds scenario, that put the third plan button below the bottom of the window. The Details toggle isn't a tab, so it now sits beside Scenarios at the top, and the tabs fit in three rows again.
