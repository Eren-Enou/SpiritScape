# v0.59: What happened

**Player problem.** This one is about the playtest more than the player. Pressing `F6` writes a play report: where your time went, how many clicks, XP a minute. That says how the game played. It doesn't say what happened in it. Did they make a spirit, name it, read the old stones, meet the last helper, reach an ending? That's most of what the last twenty milestones were about, and a playtester's notes would have to remember it all.

**Now the report says what happened.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

The report ends with a new section, **What happened**. From the reference game:

```
What happened
  Goals met: 19 of 19.
  Spirits made: 7, the first at 0:09: Ashwhisper, Fernlight, Birchsong, Emberleaf, Thistledown, Willowmere, Hazelhush.
  Keeping company: Oakroot and Fernlight; Mossbell and Thistledown.
  Old stones read: 7 of 7.
  Found on the far shore: 1 of 4.
  The last helper: met, and still waiting for an answer.
  Visitors: 10 came. You took 0 offers and turned down 0. 9 left without an answer, and 1 waiting.
  Ending: the Hearth, lit at 13:39.
```

- **A spirit you named says so:** "Fernwhistle (named by you)".
- **The ending says which one, and when.** For the Still Boat it also says how many crossed, or that they're still boarding.
- **A stone you carved, or one from an earlier game, is listed** with the line you chose and whether you found the old one.
- **Visitors are a tally.** Their names and what you said are in the notes.

### The last helper's waiting line

The reference game showed one more gap: a helper you've met but never answered wasn't mentioned at either ending. Now it is: "The last of the old helpers is still by its stone, waiting for an answer." After the boat, it's "The last of the old helpers watched the boat go. Nobody had answered it."

**What would count as failure:** the report saying something that didn't happen, or missing an ending.

## Measured

The reference game's report now ends as above. Nothing it measures changes: all 19 goals by 16:57:21, and two runs are identical.
