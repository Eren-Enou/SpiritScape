# v0.36: The Still Boat

**Player problem.** The game had one ending, so the whole of what you'd done with your spirits led to the same place. The old stones ask a real question, about someone who gave everything away and never got it back, and nothing let you answer it.

**There's now a second ending, and it's a real choice.** You can build a boat, call your spirits home, and take back everything you gave them. You'd lose them. Nobody has played it.

## Design

### The choice

| | The Hearth of Spirits | The Still Boat |
|---|---|---|
| What it says | They're someone now, so give them a home | They were you, so take yourself back |
| What you keep | Your spirits | Your levels |
| What you lose | The XP you gave them | All of them |
| Can you undo it? | It's a building | No |

### How it works

- **The Still Boat** needs the Still Lantern and a Kiln: 40 Stillwood, 20 Pitch and 20 Beams. Pitch is a new Kiln recipe at Kilnwork 9.
- **Stillwood only comes from your own hands,** and cutting it takes Woodcutting 12. If you've given that away, you have to earn it back to build the boat. I think that's the right kind of awkward.
- **Calling them takes two presses,** and the second one lists every spirit by name.
- **They finish what they're doing, put down what they carry, and walk to the shore.** They board one at a time, 2.5 seconds apart, with sparks, and each one's XP comes back as they go.
- **Spirits you never made** ("It was here before you") have nothing to give back, and they cross anyway.
- **After that the map is quiet.** You can make new spirits. The game goes on.

### Four ways it can read

The closing story depends on what you did:

- **Hearth only:** as before. If there's a boat at the shore, it says you haven't used it.
- **Boat only:** "There's no hearth here. Nobody was left who thought of it. But there's you." That answers the Last Stone.
- **Hearth, then boat:** "You lit them a hearth, and took them back anyway. It's still burning."
- **Boat, then Hearth:** "You lit the Hearth after they'd gone. Nobody stands by it."

If you've read every stone, it also says that whoever cut them never came back for what they gave. You did.

### Why spirits are only removed at the end

A spirit's position in the list is its identity, and dozens of things refer to it. Taking them out one by one would mean checking all of those. So each one is only marked as aboard, and they're all removed together once the last has boarded.

### Not a goal

The boat isn't on the list of goals, because a goal would tell you to do it. It's an offer, and a hint that the stillwood floats is all you get.

**What would count as failure:** a spirit left stranded forever, goods disappearing from the books, a crash once the list is empty, or a save that won't load afterwards.

## Measured

I ran the full reference game, paid for a boat and called everyone home:

- **9 spirits were home in 22 seconds,** from all over the map.
- **47,700 XP came back.**
- **The books balanced,** and the save loaded with no spirits.
- The reference game itself is unchanged: all 19 goals by 17:00:32.

```bash
cargo run -p spirit_sim --release --example all_goals_report -- --home
```

## Bugs this found

- **A crash on the closing screen.** Name labels still pointed at spirits that had gone. Only the UI script caught it.
- **"No spirits yet" after they'd left.** It now says they've all gone home.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **552 pass**, 539 from before plus 13 new.
- **One test changed:** an upgraded save now has seven orders, because Pitch is a new order.
- Six release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Thirty-four UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_boat.txt`, which plays the whole ending.
- The save format stays at 9, because the new fields default to empty.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- **Whether this lands at all.** It only works if the player has come to care, and no test can show that.
- Whether anyone finds the boat. It's in Projects once you have a Lantern and a Kiln, with one tip as a hint.
- The boat is a log stack and a lantern, because there's no boat model. From a distance it doesn't look much like one.
- Whether 2.5 seconds between spirits feels right.
