# v0.34: Coming back

**Player problem.** Visitors forgot you the moment they left, so a yes or a no never mattered past that one trade. Caravans also stopped bringing anything new after the third. From about the seventh hour there was nobody left to meet.

**People now come back, and what you told them follows you.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

### Visitors who remember

Each answer is now kept by name.

| Visitor | Comes when | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The pedlar, again | You took the pedlar's offer | Comes back with a bigger order |
| A surveyor's son | You turned the surveyor down | Brings what the surveyor would have left |
| The shipwright | You took the shipwright's offer, and the South Crossing stands | One last ship |
| A party of pilgrims | Always, once the foundations stand | Has heard how you treated the pilgrim |
| A mason's apprentice | Always, with a Mason's Yard | Has heard how you treated the old mason |
| A stranger with wet boots | The Still Lantern stands | Wants stillwood taken back |
| A child from the valley | The Hearth is lit | Something for after the ending |

Letting someone leave without answering counts as neither a yes nor a no. The surveyor's son says "you turned him down", and that wouldn't be true.

### Visitors no longer queue

Reading the first version showed a real flaw. Visitors came strictly in order, and four of the first five wait for a building, so the returning pedlar was stuck behind the pilgrim until the Hearth's foundations were up: hours after the 5:20 it was written for. A player who never built a Mason's Yard would never have met anyone else at all.

Now whoever is due first comes first, and someone waiting holds up nobody. At least 30 game minutes separate arrivals, so two can't land on each other's heels.

### Three more caravan finds

The fourth, fifth and sixth caravans now bring something back:

- **Oak Saplings:** three Oaks in the Starting Grove, for a player who'd rather not cross the river for them.
- **Long Racks:** 60 more room at every bench.
- **Stone Cellar:** 800 more room in Main Storage, the most of anything.

### Offers still cost something

The apprentice's offer is deliberately poor in goods: 12 Dressed Stone for 2 Keystones, when a bench makes 2 from 4 Dressed Stone and 2 Beams. You'd take it to save the time, or to be kind.

My first version gave 5, and taking every offer would then have covered the Hearth's 12 Keystones without cutting one. Before the Far Shore, visitors now give 11 at most, and a test keeps that total below what the Hearth needs.

**What would count as failure:** someone coming twice, a returning visitor who was never met, or an old save losing its place in the list.

## Measured

- **The reference game is unchanged,** because it doesn't answer visitors: all 18 goals by 16:48:59.
- **Nine of the twelve visitors come in that game,** in the order they're due. The apprentice arrives before the pilgrim, which the old queue couldn't do. The three who are missing need an answer that game never gives.

Old saves keep their count of who has come, and the new list starts empty.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **532 pass**, 518 from before plus 14 new.
- Six release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Thirty-two UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_finds.txt`.
- The save format stays at 9, because the new fields default to empty. A test loads the v8 journey fixture to show it.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- **Whether anyone notices.** Being remembered only works if the player recalls what they said hours earlier.
- Whether the words read well.
- The building label sits on bare ground for Oak Saplings, and the Stone Cellar's label can overlap Main Storage's. Both are cosmetic, and I've left them.
