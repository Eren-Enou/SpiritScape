# v0.25: The Hearth of Spirits

**Player problem.** The game had goals and no end. There was nothing to finish, and nothing that looked back at what you'd done.

**There's now a real ending, reached in 11:03 by a player who never hesitates.** A person will take much longer. Nobody has played it, so whether it lands is unknown.

## Design

Every spirit you've made came out of you, so the last thing you build is a place for them.

- **Three stages,** in order, in the Projects tab (`B`):
  - **Hearth Foundations:** Stone, Dressed Stone and beams.
  - **Hearth Walls:** Logs, Panels and beams.
  - **Hearth of Spirits:** Keystones, Panels and Dressed Stone.
- **It needs everything:** all three trades, and the Mason's Yard first. A test checks the costs cover every chain.
- **Your Story:** a new card at the top of the Progress tab (`J`). Before the end it hints at what's ahead. Afterwards it tells how long it took, how much XP you gave up, what your best-known spirits did, and how many caravans went out.
- **The game goes on.** Lighting the Hearth ends the story, not the save.
- **New goal:** Light the Hearth of Spirits, making 16.

### Rejected

- **A hard stop or a credits screen:** people may have goals left, and a settlement with nothing to do is a poor reward.
- **A reward for finishing:** there's nothing left to spend it on.

**What would count as failure:** the Hearth being impossible to finish in a long run, or lighting it stopping the game.

## Measured

Reproduce with `cargo run -p spirit_sim --release --example all_goals_report`.

| One long game | Met |
|---|---|
| Forest Crossing | under 2:00 |
| Master Woodworking | 8:25 |
| Cut 10 Keystones | 10:48 |
| **Light the Hearth of Spirits** | **11:03** |
| Master Woodcutting | 13:11 |

All 16 goals are met. The reference player reacts within a minute and never hesitates, and it knows the whole route. The target of 20 hours for a new player looks plausible but is a guess.

The ending text is a list of facts about your game. I can't judge if it moves anyone.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **464 pass**, 458 from before plus 6 in `tests/ending.rs`.
- **One count changed:** 16 goals, in one test and one UI script.
- Six release-only long-run tests pass, including every goal in one game.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Twenty-four UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_ending.txt`. I inspected a screenshot of the ending.
- The save format stays at 8. New fields have defaults.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- Physical mouse and keyboard.
- **Whether the ending feels like one.** It's a card and a message, with no special animation or sound.
