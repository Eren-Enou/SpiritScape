# v0.27: Seasons

**Player problem.** Every hour of the game was like every other. Nothing changed what was worth doing.

**A real game now has a year.** The woods grow twice as fast in spring and stop in winter, which pushes you toward the quarry and the bench. Adding it also exposed a serious bug: a bench could stop working for good. Nobody has played this.

## Design

| Season | Length | Woods |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | 30 minutes | Regrow twice as fast |
| Summer | 30 minutes | As usual |
| Autumn | 30 minutes | As usual |
| Winter | 30 minutes | Stop regrowing |

- **Data:** `[[seasons]]`, and `seasonal` on tree types. Stone Outcrops don't follow the seasons.
- **From the clock:** the season comes from the game time, so there's nothing to save.
- **Real games only:** scenarios and the fast development profile have no seasons, so everything they measure stands.
- **On screen:** the season shows beside the speed, and each change gets a message that says what it means.

### Rejected

- **A frozen river you can walk over:** it's a lovely idea, but it means changing the map under walkers twice a year. It's worth trying if seasons prove popular.

## The bug it exposed

The guided player stopped dead, and stayed stopped for six game hours. The cause had nothing to do with seasons, which only changed the timing.

- **A bench could lock up.** Planks set aside for your own training sat on the bench's shelf and counted as finished goods, so the shelf looked full and the worker stopped. A hauler wouldn't take them because they were reserved. Any player who chose Fitted Panels at an outpost bench and walked away could hit this.
- **A related risk from v0.14:** a hauler with a big enough bag could wait for a full load larger than a shelf can ever hold.

Both are fixed, with a test each.

## Measured

Reproduce with `cargo run -p spirit_sim --release --example all_goals_report`.

| One long game | v0.26 | v0.27 |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Crossing, message-only player | 1:46 | 2:17 |
| Master Woodworking | 8:25 | 10:38 |
| Light the Hearth of Spirits | 11:03 | 14:10 |
| Every goal | 13:12 | 16:20 |
| Left behind | 392 | 112 |

Seasons add about a quarter to the length of a game. Winter is a quarter of the year with no wood, and this reference player doesn't plan around it, so a person who does may lose less.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace`: **483 pass**, 472 from before plus 9 in `tests/seasons.rs` and 2 regression tests.
- **One limit loosened:** a 45-minute session may now spend under 3% of its time with a full bag, up from 2%. Spring's faster regrowth fills the bag sooner: it measured 2.3%.
- Six release-only long-run tests pass.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean.
- **Twenty-six UI scripts pass,** including new `ui_seasons.txt`.
- The save format stays at 8.
- The repo has no CI, so all of this ran locally.

## Not verified

- Physical mouse and keyboard.
- **Whether winter feels like a change of plan, or just a 30-minute penalty.**
- There's no visual change on the map yet. Snow or autumn colours would help a lot.
