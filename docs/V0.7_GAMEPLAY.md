# v0.7: Skilling Experience and Gameplay Validation

The question for this version: can a player enjoy 30 to 60 minutes of training while their spirits run a useful settlement?

**The measurements say the session now works mechanically. They can't say whether it's enjoyable.** A reference player gets through a 46-minute session under normal rules at about 2 interactions a minute, with almost no time stuck. Nobody has played it yet, so the checklist at the end is the next step.

No skills, resources or production chains were added.

## Results at a glance

All numbers are from the normal profile with the reference player, and they repeat exactly. "v0.6 behavior" means automatic targeting off, which matches the v0.6 baseline for Woodcutting. The full report is in [V0.7_PACING.md](V0.7_PACING.md).

| Measure | v0.6 behavior | v0.7 |
|---|---|---|
| Clicks to regain 1,800 Woodcutting XP | 38 | **9** |
| Tree clicks over a 46-minute session | 283 | **34** |
| Clicks to regain 1,800 Woodworking XP with spirit support | 13 | **2** |
| Time stuck with a full bag, if the player follows the storage advice | not measured | **5 seconds of 46 minutes** |
| Same, if the bot ignores the advice | | 50% of a 106-minute run |

Continuous gathering doesn't change the pace of the first cycle much: 22:28 with it, 21:58 without. It saves clicks, not time.

## Gathering

Click a tree and leave it. You keep chopping until something ends the session.

| Rule | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Next tree | Same kind you clicked first, then the same material, nearest first |
| Qualified only | Trees above your level are never chosen |
| Site | Stays inside the site you started in |
| Reach | At most 12 tiles of walking per move (`gather_reach_tiles`) |
| Bag full | Stops and says where to unload |
| Moving, clicking elsewhere, `X` | Stops at once |
| Nothing standing | Waits up to 120 seconds for regrowth, then stops and says why |
| A spirit finishes your tree | You move on; it isn't treated as an error |
| Automatic targeting off | Stops when the tree is finished |

- **Where you can see it:** the Now card shows what you're doing, and a gold square marks the tree.
- **No offline XP:** a session only runs while the game does. Loading never simulates time away.
- **Default:** on in real games. The older development scenarios keep one click per tree, because their tests and scripts depend on it. The new fast scenarios start with it on.

## Woodworking

Choose a recipe in the Training tab, or step through them with the Now card's **Making** button.

- **Best available** is the old behavior and still the default.
- **A chosen recipe is the only thing you make.** Level 10 no longer forces Structural Beams.
- **Each recipe shows** its inputs, output, XP, XP a minute, level and what you're short of, so you can choose between fast training and a particular product.
- **Empty hands at a storage** take what the chosen recipe needs.
- **Repeats** until something runs out, then says which: materials, room, level or a place at the bench.
- **Changing recipe mid-batch** discards that batch's progress, so it gives no free time.
- **Walking away** stops work, and an unfinished batch costs nothing.

## Training support

The Training tab now offers three named ways to train. Each one just sets the existing options.

| Mode | What it sets | XP/min | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manual** | No support | 455 | 16 |
| **Relaxed** | Collect and supply, production first | 357 | 2 |
| **Optimized**, nothing built yet | Also training first, a bench place, one hauler and one gatherer on your work | 267 | 2 |
| **Optimized**, after 6:33 of building up | Plus a Log Rack and a standing stock | **734** | **1** |

These are the times to saw 1,800 Woodworking XP back from the same moment.

**Optimized is the slowest mode until you invest, and the game says so.** Its text says it's "slower than Relaxed until it is" set up, and the tab lists what's done and what's missing. That's the trade the spec asked for, but a player who picks it early may still feel punished. It's worth watching in playtesting.

A bench session now waits up to 60 seconds for spirits instead of ending, so Relaxed really is one click. If nothing comes, it stops and gives the reason. The tab also names the current limit, for example "Logs supply is the limit: you use about 40 a minute, gatherers bring about 13".

Spirits still only carry. A test confirms an idle player and every spirit gain no XP under Optimized.

## Readability

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Activity was a small note beside the bag | **Now** card at the top: activity, state, session numbers, reason, Stop |
| Soul Separation always filled the panel | Its own tab, `R` |
| Each skill took four or more lines | One bar and a "Next" line. Full unlock chips are in Progress |
| You had to open tabs to see if things worked | **Settlement** card: worker counts, production, storage, the one thing needing attention |
| Scenario buttons took three rows | Behind a **Scenarios** button |
| Time charts and planner detail always showed | Behind **Details** (`V`) |
| A command's reply stayed up forever | Clears after 6 seconds |

The Soul and Training tabs hide the You and Settlement cards while open, so they need less scrolling. The Training tab still runs past the bottom of the default 920-pixel window. The tab system stays, and this isn't a UI rewrite.

## Progress feedback

- **Unlocks** now say where, what and why, for example: "Oak: in Starting Grove and Distant Forest. 5 Logs a tree, 30 XP each. Twice the XP of a Tree for each log..."
- **Longer notices** stay up longer.
- **Soul Separation** says what you lost and that the spirit keeps it for good.
- **A locked tree** names a spirit that can cut it.
- **The preview** adds why the tier matters.

## Pacing

| Finding | Number |
|---|---|
| Woodcutting XP earned in 46 minutes | 14,805, including the 1,800 given up |
| Toward level 10 at 46 minutes | 12,600 of 16,000 |
| Chopping and spending nothing | Warned at 13:10, **stuck at 15:29 with 5,370 XP** |
| Doing what the warning says | 5 seconds stuck in 46 minutes |

So the earlier estimate of about 40 minutes to level 10 is low once a spirit has been made: expect closer to an hour for the reference player, and longer for a person.

**The storage wall is real, and it's now a decision instead of a dead end.** No capacity or XP numbers changed. The game warns once, suggests only what's possible right now (saw logs, raise the plank target, order a Storehouse), and What's Next repeats it. The reference player solved it with those options alone.

## Diagnostics

The play log is part of the save, so a report covers a whole playthrough across sessions. It records time gathering, processing, moving, waiting and stuck, interactions by type, XP per skill, spirit output while you train, and how long sacrificed levels took to regain. Nothing reads it to make a decision, and a test confirms it doesn't change play.

To see it, turn on Details and open Progress, or press `F6`.

## Saves

- The format is now **version 3**, adding only fields with safe defaults, so no migration was needed.
- **Sessions save exactly:** tests cover saving while chopping, walking between trees, waiting for regrowth, at the bench, and waiting for spirits. Each continues identically to a world that was never saved.
- **v0.6 saves load:** `tests/fixtures/v2_*.save` were written by the unmodified v0.6 code. A job under way finishes as one task and the player then stands idle, because older saves have no session to continue.
- **v0.5 saves still load**, and the original of an upgraded save is kept as `world.save.v2`.

## What still needs a person

- Physical mouse and keyboard. All input so far was scripted.
- Whether anything is relaxing, clear or fun. **Passing tests don't show that.**
- How long a person takes. The reference player never hesitates.
- Whether the new layout is actually easier to read.

## Known problems

- **The settlement is quiet.** Spirits were busy only 11% of the time while the reference player trained, because stock targets are met quickly. They cut 100 logs and made 66 goods in 44 minutes. It runs on its own, but it may not feel alive.
- **Reference numbers vary by setup.** Manual sawing measured 455 XP/min with targeting on and 521 with it off. The bot spent 26% of the first run chopping and 9% of the second, probably because a chop trip now fills the bag rather than stopping after a tree. The two aren't a clean comparison.
- **A planned building can refuse yours.** Nonstop chopping can trigger a Field Storage just as you order a Storehouse, and "finish the current project first" doesn't say which project.

## Technical debt

- Hauling Spirits only bring ordinary Logs to the bench, so Structural Beams get no training support. The session stops and says what's missing.
- Bench stock still lives only at the first Workshop.
- `recipe_options` reads the first Workshop too.
- Choosing a training mode writes several log lines.
- `training_first` doesn't stop production from taking logs. That's part of why Optimized starts slow.
- The Training tab still needs scrolling at the default window size.
- The scenario `auto_gather` flag exists only to keep older tests valid.

## Recommendations for v0.8

1. **Run the playtest below before building anything else.** Its answers should set the priorities.
2. **Give the settlement more to do during training.** Higher default targets or standing uses for planks would help with the 11% figure.
3. **Decide whether Optimized should start slow.** Letting training-first hold logs back from production may be enough.
4. **Add uses for goods.** Storage is only a real choice while there's something worth spending on.
5. **Generalize bench support** beyond Logs.

Press `F6` when you finish, and keep `play_report.txt` from your save folder.

**Woodcutting**
- [ ] Does it feel relaxing? Could you look away for a minute?
- [ ] Did the next tree it picked ever surprise you?
- [ ] Did it ever stop when you didn't expect it to, or carry on when you wanted it to stop?
- [ ] Did `X`, walking and clicking elsewhere all stop it as you'd expect?

**Woodworking**
- [ ] Could you find and choose a recipe without help?
- [ ] Did you understand why a recipe couldn't be made?
- [ ] Did you choose differently for XP and for materials?

**Spirits**
- [ ] Did your first spirit feel worth the XP you gave up?
- [ ] Did you notice the settlement helping you? When?
- [ ] Which of Manual, Relaxed and Optimized did you prefer, and why?
- [ ] Did Optimized feel like a goal, or a trap?

**The settlement**
- [ ] Could production run without you correcting it?
- [ ] When something stopped, did you know why?
- [ ] Did the storage warning help, and did you know what to do?
- [ ] Did the settlement feel alive, or idle?

**Overall**
- [ ] Did the panel show what you wanted without scrolling?
- [ ] After your first settlement improvement, did you want to keep training?
- [ ] How long did you play? When did you first want to stop?
- [ ] Which clicks still felt like chores?
