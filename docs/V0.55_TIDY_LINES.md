# v0.55: Tidy lines

**Player problem.** In the Training tab, each recipe was one long line:

> 2 Logs -> 1 Planks, 25 XP  (714 XP/min, 28 made/
> min)

Every recipe wrapped, and the text broke at the slash, leaving "min)" alone on a line. The line above it did the same: "You use 0 Logs/min | gatherers bring 28/min | 40" and then "spare" on its own. It's the tab you read when choosing what to train, and it looked broken.

**Now each line breaks where it reads naturally.** Nobody has played it.

## Design

- **Each recipe takes two short lines instead of one long one:** "2 Logs -> 1 Plank", with "25 XP each" on the right, and then "714 XP/min, 28 made a minute".
- **"You use 0 Logs/min" and "gatherers bring 28/min" share a line, left and right,** and "40 spare" goes below.
- "XP/min" stays as it was, because two UI scripts look for it and it never wrapped badly. Only "made/min" became "made a minute".

The tab is the same height as before, because the long lines were already taking two rows each. They just break in the right places now.

**What would count as failure:** a recipe line that still wraps, or a number on a line away from what it counts.
